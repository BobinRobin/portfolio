What the business gets

⛴
Logistics

Goods do not wait at the border

Marking and documents are prepared automatically. By the time freight reaches customs, the whole package is ready — delays and their costs are removed.

▦
Procurement

No manual QR ordering

The loop requests codes and tracks their statuses itself. Manual ordering and registry reconciliation are gone.

✎
Documents

UPDs are built automatically

XML generation follows the contract, limits and ordering — by code, not by hand. A signed UTD is produced without a human in the loop.

₽
Revenue

Profit recognition

~129,000 units reach the sale stage: a signed UTD plus the sale status is the moment a deal can be booked. The loop gets there by itself.

Reliability as savings

Zero failure-status counters and single retries are not luck — they come from idempotency, a gate before writes, and dry-run channels. Every prevented failure is saved sales days and unspent hours of manual triage.

Honest about numbers

"Measured" — Prometheus counters, Redis queue lengths, worker logs (processing time). "Estimated" — any money amounts: exact financials are not disclosed in a public portfolio. Figures are rounded and generalized without corporate identifiers.
