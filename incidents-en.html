Incidents

Real production failures. Each one: symptom, cause, fix, what changed.

Stickers: new codes were not sent to FTP

Symptom. New records were written to the DB, but XML was not sent. Log line: skip_no_pd=1000.

Cause. The product date was taken only from existing rows; new codes were inserted without it, even though dates existed in another table.

Fix. Extended the batch processor to look up the date from the correct source for codes without one.

Result. Codes flow to FTP again; the "data present, no send" mismatch is gone.

Idempotency: a wrong key duplicated records

Symptom. Re-processing created duplicates because the idempotency key was not unique per document.

Fix. Switched deduplication to a truly unique key and verified the re-post scenario.

Result. Re-posts stop duplicating; retries are safe.

Grafana could not reach Loki

Symptom. After assembling the observability stack, Grafana could not fetch logs.

Cause. A proxy conflict on the server.

Fix. Moved Grafana to host network with a loopback datasource; switched the log agent to journald with a unit label.

Result. Logs are visible in Grafana and filterable per worker.

Go-live blockers (critical defects)

Symptom. A source audit found defects that blocked the production rollout.

Fix. Fixed async logic in the FTP poller (races, lost results), a mismatched callback contract, chunking-limit bypass, disabled idempotency, and exposed secrets (moved to a secret store).

Result. The loop reached a safe state; external channels are enabled one at a time.

Observability from scratch

Symptom. No metrics; worker logs were read manually.

Fix. Assembled Prometheus + Grafana + Loki: RED metrics, business status counters, two SRE dashboards.

Result. Diagnosis is no longer blind: incidents are visible in metrics and logs.

Conclusions

  • Idempotency from day one — the key must be unique.
  • Enable external channels one at a time.
  • Observability before the incident, not after.
